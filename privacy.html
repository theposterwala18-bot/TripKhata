Privacy Policy • Last updated 24 September 2026

What TripKhata does

TripKhata helps users manage trip expenses, customer khata and supplier records, reports and optional cloud synchronization.

Information we process

  • Account information such as name, email address, Firebase user ID and sign-in provider.
  • Optional profile information such as phone number, bio and profile photo.
  • App records you create, including trips, members, expenses, settlements, customer/supplier entries and notes.
  • Optional receipt or bill images you attach to entries.
  • If you use contact picking on a supported device, TripKhata only receives the contact you explicitly select.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide TripKhata features such as saving your records, restoring them across devices, generating reports and syncing your account. We do not sell your personal information.

Cloud services

TripKhata uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for sign-in and cloud synchronization. Data sent to Firebase is associated with your authenticated account.

Local storage

Some data may also be stored on your device/browser so the app can work quickly and offline. Signing out does not automatically erase local records unless you delete them or delete your account.

Data retention and deletion

Your cloud data is retained while your account remains active. You can request or perform account deletion from within TripKhata or from the account deletion page below. Deleting your account is intended to remove your TripKhata account and associated TripKhata cloud data.

Security

TripKhata uses authenticated Firebase access and user-specific Firestore rules. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their Google/email account and device.
